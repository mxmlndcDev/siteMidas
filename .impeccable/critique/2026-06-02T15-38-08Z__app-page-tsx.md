## Overall Impression

A typographically confident landing page that has genuine personality. The clip-wipe hero entrance and "Premium." in gold italic create a signature moment. The weakest surface by far is the form — it collects PII for a gambling club membership but produces no feedback on submit. That's the conversion point and it's broken. Everything else is strong.

## What's Working

**1. The hero entrance is the brand.** The staggered clip-wipe on "Experi-encia / Premium." is exactly how to launch a premium brand page. It takes your time, it demands attention, and "Premium." landing in gold italic is the kind of typographic payoff that justifies a design system.

**2. Spatial discipline via border-grid.** Using `border-border/20` horizontal dividers as the layout skeleton (metadata strip, heading section, card grid, stats strip) creates editorial rhythm without needing a traditional container/column structure. Every section is a composition, not a template slot.

**3. Copy-design integration.** "Donde más ven límites, nosotros vemos *posibilidades.*" with the italic emphasis on the key word is exactly the kind of considered choice that separates brand design from UI work. The tier descriptions ("Atención VIP, tragos y valet parking") are lean and concrete — no adjective fluff.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Form has no success or error state**
- **Why it matters**: Users click "Quiero ser parte" and the form POSTs to a placeholder URL. Either a broken page loads, or nothing visible happens. This is the primary conversion action — zero feedback at this moment costs every sign-up.
- **Fix**: Replace the `action`/`method` POST with a controlled `onSubmit` handler. Add a `submitted` state that shows a confirmation message. Add a `submitting` state that disables the button.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] No 18+ age validation**
- **Why it matters**: The date field collects birth date, but no client-side check validates the user is at least 18. For a gambling membership this is a legal/regulatory requirement.
- **Fix**: On form submit, compute age from the date field. If under 18, block submit and show inline: "Debés ser mayor de 18 años para unirte al Club Midas."
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Sede cards have no interaction affordance**
- **Why it matters**: The 2×2 grid scale-on-hover implies clickability, but clicking does nothing. Users interested in a specific sede are stranded — no address detail, no hours, no link to directions.
- **Fix**: Either add a Google Maps link per sede (href on the card), or add a hover overlay that reveals address/hours. Currently the hover feedback creates a false affordance.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Missing skip-to-content link**
- **Why it matters**: WCAG 2.4.1 (AA). Keyboard users must Tab through the nav on every page load. The `mix-blend-difference` nav compounds this since it persists over all content.
- **Fix**: First child of `<body>`: `<a href="#hero-section" className="sr-only focus:not-sr-only focus:fixed focus:top-2 focus:left-2 focus:z-[200] focus:bg-bg focus:text-blue focus:px-4 focus:py-2 focus:text-xs focus:uppercase focus:tracking-widest">Saltar al contenido</a>`
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Stats strip lacks semantic structure**
- **Why it matters**: "1000+", "PUESTOS", "4", "SALAS" are sibling spans — screen readers announce them as a stream of unrelated tokens, not as labeled statistics.
- **Fix**: Wrap each pair: `<div role="group" aria-label="Más de 1000 puestos"><span>1000+</span><span>Puestos</span></div>` or use a `<dl>/<dt>/<dd>` structure.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (First-Timer)** — arrives via Google search for "bingo Ciudadela". Reads "Experiencia Premium." — premium what? There is no above-fold explanation of what Club Midas is before asking to sign up. The tier list is the first concrete explanation and it's below the fold. Jordan may convert to the form without understanding what they're joining, increasing form abandonment.
- After clicking "Quiero ser parte": silence. Jordan has no idea if registration succeeded. This is the highest abandonment-risk moment in the entire funnel.

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent)** — the heading structure is now correct (single h1 ✓). Menu has `role="dialog"` and `aria-modal` ✓. However: focus trap only sets initial focus — tabbing past "Cerrar" escapes into the underlying page. Stats strip reads as 6 disconnected text nodes. Sede location text `text-white/60` over a gradient may still fail 4.5:1 in lighter gradient zones.

## Minor Observations

- The overlay menu has 2 links (Sedes, Club Midas) but the bottom CTA also reads "Unirse gratis → Club Midas." These two paths to the same destination feel redundant; the bottom CTA could link to something distinct (e.g., Instagram, WhatsApp, or a phone number).
- The form's date input renders with native browser chrome that will look different on iOS vs Android vs desktop. A custom date picker styled to the system would feel more premium.
- "Tarritos de regalo" — "tarrito" is regional. Consider whether this reads to all Argentine provinces served.

## Questions to Consider

- "What happens after a successful sign-up? Email confirmation, WhatsApp message, nothing? The form currently promises an experience it can't deliver without knowing the answer."
- "Should sede cards link somewhere? Google Maps is free and solves the 'how do I get there' question that every first-visit customer has."
- "The landing has no event section even though the hero mentions 'Aniversario Ciudadela · Vie 27'. Is the event info supposed to live here? That's a time-sensitive conversion driver being wasted."
